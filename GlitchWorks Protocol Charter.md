## **ARTICLE 1: THE NON-ASSIGNMENT SHIELD (COPYRIGHT & MORAL RIGHTS)**

### **1.1 Ultimate Ownership**

The Creator is, and shall permanently remain, the sole author and legal owner of the copyright for all works routed through this Protocol. Pursuant to the Estonian Copyright Act, this protection lasts for the life of the Creator plus seventy (70) years.

### **1.2 Inalienable Moral Rights**

Under no circumstances shall this Agreement be construed as a waiver or transfer of the Creator's Moral Rights. The Creator permanently retains the right of authorship, the right of integrity, and the right of protection of their honor and reputation.

### **1.3 The Economic Mandate**

The Creator grants the Operator an exclusive, indefinite license strictly limited to the management, routing, and B2B licensing of specified **Economic Rights** (e.g., synchronization, distribution, commercial use) as defined in the Creator's specific Repository/Catalogue.

## **ARTICLE 2: THE TRI-STATE EXECUTION PROTOCOL**

This Agreement operates continuously ("valid until dissolved") and is governed by a programmable state machine logic. The license exists in one of three states:

### **2.1 \[STATE: ACTIVE\]**

The Operator actively markets, negotiates, and executes B2B deals on behalf of the Creator's catalogue. All inbound revenue is routed according to the 30/70 Financial Protocol (Article 3).

### **2.2 \[STATE: SUSPENDED\] (The Cooldown Hatch)**

Triggered unilaterally by the Creator via written notice or cryptographic signature.

> * **Effect:** The Operator immediately ceases initiating or executing *new* B2B commercial deals.  
> * **Legacy:** The Creator's infrastructure remains deployed. Previously executed deals continue to generate revenue and route through the 30/70 Protocol until their natural expiration.  
> * **Freedom:** The Creator is free to independently secure deals or sign with third parties outside the Glitch Works infrastructure while in this state.

### **2.3 \[STATE: DISSOLVED\] (The Kill-Switch)**

Triggered by mutual agreement, uncured material breach, or specific invocation by the Creator.

> * **Effect:** Total legal severing of the mandate.  
> * **Reversion:** All unexploited Economic Rights automatically and immediately revert to the Creator.  
> * **Data Scrubber:** All non-essential operational and personal data is permanently deleted in accordance with EU GDPR standards, excluding data legally required for financial auditing.
